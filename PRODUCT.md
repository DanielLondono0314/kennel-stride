# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Three roles share one app, none clearly primary:

- **Owner / admin** of the kennel: runs operations, finances, reports, staff. Wants control and a business-level view.
- **Front-desk / care staff**: check-in/out, reservations, report cards, daily repetitive tasks. Wants speed and zero friction.
- **Pet owners (customers)**: self-service for reservations, report cards, invoices. Wants trust and simplicity.

Context: a working dog boarding / daycare facility. Staff use the app on the floor, often quickly, sometimes one-handed. Owners review at a desk. Customers check in occasionally from a phone.

## Product Purpose

KennelStride is operational software for dog boarding / daycare businesses: reservations, check-in/out, dog and customer records, clinic/medical info, facility config, billing, invoices, reports, campaigns, report cards, and staff management.

Success = the facility runs faster and makes fewer mistakes than it would on paper or generic tools, and everyone (staff, owners, customers) trusts the data.

## Brand Personality

**Warm but professional.** Calm, competent, pet-friendly without being childish. The voice of a serious small business that genuinely cares about the animals. Three words: warm, capable, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid all of these:

- **Generic AI SaaS dashboard**: identical card grids, hero-metric template, random gradients, "made by AI" look.
- **Dated veterinary software**: dense gray tables, 2000s-era UX, ugly forms.
- **Too childish**: overload of pet emojis / cartoon colors that undercut the business.
- **Cold corporate**: banking navy with no warmth.

The needle sits between dated-vet and cold-corporate on one axis, and between childish and generic-SaaS on the other. Warm and human, but a real operational tool.

## Design Principles

1. **Speed is a feature.** Repetitive staff tasks (check-in, reservations) must be fast and low-friction. Optimize the common path.
2. **Critical data is sacred.** Medication, allergies, aggression flags, billing: surface them clearly, make them hard to get wrong.
3. **Warm, not cute.** Humanity and care through tone, spacing, and color, not through mascots or emoji noise.
4. **Earn trust through clarity.** Customers and staff should always know what's happening, what's confirmed, and what's pending.
5. **One app, three jobs.** Respect that owner, staff, and customer each have a different primary task; don't force one role's UI onto another.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: AA contrast, full keyboard operability, visible focus, labelled controls and forms, respect for reduced-motion. Account for quick/distracted use on the floor and occasional phone use by customers.
